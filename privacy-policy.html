This Privacy Policy describes how Daniel Clay (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and protects information in connection with SMS / text message alerts, including personal weather digests and other alerts you request.

Information we collect

  • Mobile phone number you provide when you ask to receive SMS alerts
  • SMS opt-in / opt-out status and related consent records
  • Message content you send to our number (for example STOP, HELP, START, YES)
  • Basic delivery metadata (timestamps, delivery status) from our messaging provider

How we use information

  • To send the SMS alerts you requested (typically up to two weather digests per day, plus occasional alerts only when you ask)
  • To process opt-in, opt-out (STOP), and help (HELP) requests
  • To comply with carrier and A2P 10DLC messaging rules

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except with messaging providers used solely to deliver our SMS service.

We use Twilio as our messaging provider to deliver texts. Twilio processes phone numbers and message data only to send and receive messages on our behalf and to meet carrier compliance rules.
